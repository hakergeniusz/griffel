# Welcome to Griffel

Griffel is a distraction-free Markdown editor. This short guide walks you
through the essentials. Feel free to edit anything here — this document
starts unsaved, so nothing can go wrong.

## Writing

Just type. Your text lives in a centered column; the interface stays out of
your way while you write and returns when you need it.

**Bold**, *italic*, ~~strikethrough~~ and `inline code` are highlighted as
you type them.

## Structure

- Bulleted lists continue automatically when you press Enter.
- Numbered lists increment their numbers.
- Checklists reset their checkbox:

- [ ] Try pressing Enter here.

Headings, block quotes and horizontal rules are styled in place:

> Block quotes hang into the margin.

---

## Preview

Press **Ctrl+P** to toggle the live preview. You can arrange it beside,
below, or instead of the editor, or in its own window.

## Focus

Two writing modes help you concentrate:

- **Focus Mode** (Ctrl+D) dims everything but the sentence you are writing.
- **Hemingway Mode** (Ctrl+T) blocks deletions so you keep moving forward.

## Files and export

Save with **Ctrl+S**. If the app ever crashes, your unsaved work is restored
from automatic snapshots the next time it starts. Export to PDF, HTML, ODT
and more from the menu next to the Save button.

Happy writing!
